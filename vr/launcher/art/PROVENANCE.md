# The launcher's art

The project's own art, made for HookshotVR (20 Nov 2026), with nothing of Nintendo's left in it:

- `frame.png`: the leather frame `assets/vr/panel_frame.png` (1670 × 941), with the ornament in the middle of its bottom
  band (two sweeps and a Triforce, x 640-1030, y 818-900) covered by the plain band from just left of it, the same rows.
- `wordmark.png`: the HOOKSHOTVR wordmark and its subtitle line, cut from the 1920 × 1080 title picture on the design
  canvas (x 596-1820, y 373-704), which leaves out the figure on its left; white, with its brightness as the alpha.

Both were checked at full size for any Link, Triforce or other Nintendo mark before they were added.
